Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment

A vendor-security assessment case study prepared to evaluate how third-party access, data handling, security controls, incident response and resilience affect organizational risk.

Objective

Determine whether a prospective or existing vendor introduces unacceptable cybersecurity risk and identify controls required before or during engagement.

Planned Deliverables

  • Vendor security questionnaire
  • Data/access classification
  • Risk findings register
  • Control-gap analysis
  • Compensating controls
  • Onboarding recommendation

Hands-on Evidence

Pending practical training. Findings will not be represented as completed until supporting lab evidence is collected.

Assessment Areas

Governance and security ownership • access control • data protection • vulnerability management • incident response • business continuity • third-party dependencies • evidence and assurance.

Decision Model

Understand vendor service and data exposure → assess controls → identify gaps → rate risk → define treatment or compensating controls → document an evidence-based onboarding decision.

Status

Assessment template prepared; hands-on validation pending.
