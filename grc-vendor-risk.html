GRC PORTFOLIO • COMPLETED HANDS-ON SIMULATION

FinSecure Banking — Third-Party Risk Management & PCI DSS Assessment

A hands-on third-party risk management simulation in which I acted as the TPRM lead for a financial institution onboarding a payment processor with direct access to the cardholder-data environment. The engagement required vendor due diligence, SOC 2 Type II assurance review, PCI DSS-aligned control verification and issue documentation.

Scope

Vendor profiling, criticality classification, vendor-risk assessment, PCI DSS control verification, control-effectiveness assessment and issue/remediation tracking.

Completed Work

  • Created 1 critical vendor profile
  • Assessed 4 third-party security risks
  • Verified 6 PCI DSS-aligned controls
  • Documented 3 security/process issues with owners and due dates

Outcome

Completed all 4 project tasks with 5 controls rated Effective, 1 Partial and 3 open issues documented for remediation.

Vendor Profile

SecurePay Solutions — simulated critical third-party payment processor with direct access to the cardholder-data environment. Assessment notes captured PCI DSS due diligence requirements and the need to verify SOC 2 Type II attestation.

Vendor Risk Highlights

Compromised Vendor Credentials

Compromised payment-processor credentials enable unauthorized access and exposure of cardholder data. Likelihood 4 × Impact 5 = Critical.

Inadequate Data Protection

Weak protection of payment data during processing, storage or transmission creates breach and PCI DSS exposure.

Payment Service Outage

Third-party service failure disrupts payment processing, affecting availability, revenue and customer operations.

Compliance & Assurance Gap

Failure to validate PCI DSS compliance and SOC 2 Type II assurance could leave material vendor control weaknesses undetected.

PCI DSS Controls Verified

  • 8.4.2: MFA for privileged access to the cardholder-data environment.
  • 4.2.1: strong cryptography for cardholder data in transit.
  • 7.2.2: least-privilege and business-need access for third parties; assessed as Partial to reflect review-evidence weakness.
  • 10.2.1: logging and monitoring of access to cardholder data and critical systems.
  • 7.2.1: role-based/business-need access restriction.
  • 5.2.1: anti-malware protection for applicable systems.

Issues & Remediation

Privileged Access Review Gap

High-severity process gap: privileged-access reviews were not consistently performed and documented. Remediation: scheduled reviews, documented approvals and timely removal of unnecessary privileges.

Security Log Review Gap

High-severity process gap: log and alert reviews were not consistently evidenced. Remediation: defined monitoring intervals, ownership and retained review evidence.

Weak Encryption Protocols

High-severity security gap: legacy/weak protocols permitted for cardholder-data transmission. Remediation: disable insecure protocols and enforce strong cryptography.
