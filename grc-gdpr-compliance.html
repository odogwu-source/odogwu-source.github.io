GRC PORTFOLIO • COMPLETED HANDS-ON SIMULATION

EuroShop GmbH — GDPR Compliance Programme Implementation

A hands-on privacy and GRC simulation for a fictional e-commerce organisation operating across EU member states and processing personal data for more than three million customers. The exercise focused on data-processing asset mapping, privacy-risk assessment, control design and accountable remediation.

Scope

GDPR-aligned asset inventory, privacy risk register, data protection controls, accountability and remediation planning.

Key Work

  • Mapped 5 production data-processing assets
  • Assessed 6 privacy risks
  • Implemented 10 controls
  • Connected risks to owners, treatment actions and governance responsibilities

Environment

Simulated EU e-commerce operation handling customer, employee, payment and marketing data.

Data-Processing Assets

  • Customer Database: customer identities, contact details, account information and order history.
  • E-Commerce Web Application: customer-facing application used for accounts, orders and personal-data collection.
  • Payment Processing System: payment transactions and associated financial/personal information.
  • Employee HR Management System: employee records, payroll-related information and HR data.
  • Customer CRM & Marketing Platform: contact information, preferences, consent and engagement history.

Privacy Risk Examples

Unauthorized Customer-Data Access

Weak access controls could expose customer personal information. Likelihood 4 × Impact 5 = Critical.

Phishing & Credential Compromise

Employee susceptibility to phishing could enable unauthorized access to customer information. Likelihood 4 × Impact 4 = High.

Unencrypted Personal Data

Insufficient encryption could expose sensitive customer information. Likelihood 3 × Impact 5 = High.

Third-Party Data Protection

Vendor control weaknesses could lead to exposure of customer data and accountability failures.

Skills Demonstrated

GDPR governance • data inventory • privacy-risk assessment • asset classification • access control • data protection • vendor risk • accountability • remediation planning • security documentation.

Portfolio Integrity

This is an educational hands-on simulation, not a production GDPR audit or legal compliance opinion. The organisation and records are simulated and the project demonstrates practical privacy-GRC reasoning.
