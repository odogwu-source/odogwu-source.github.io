Portfolio Method

Across the projects, I use a consistent workflow: understand the business scenario → identify assets and data → identify risks or incidents → assess likelihood, impact and severity → select and document controls → assign ownership → collect evidence → track issues or treatment actions → document lessons learned and outcomes.
