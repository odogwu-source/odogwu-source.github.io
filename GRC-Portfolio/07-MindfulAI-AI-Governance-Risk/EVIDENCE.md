# Project 07 Evidence Index — MindfulAI AI Governance & Responsible AI Risk

This evidence index documents the final curated screenshots retained for the portfolio case study. The evidence originates from an educational GRC simulation and is presented as training evidence rather than production assurance evidence.

## Final Recruiter-Facing Evidence Set

1. `01-ai-asset-inventory-summary.png` — AI asset inventory summary showing five production AI systems, with three Critical and two High classifications.
2. `02-ai-asset-inventory-table.png` — completed AI asset inventory table demonstrating ownership, classification and production status.
3. `03-critical-ai-risk-assessment.png` — Critical AI model-bias risk assessment with likelihood-impact scoring and documented rationale.
4. `04-data-protection-risk-assessment.png` — Critical sensitive-data exposure risk demonstrating privacy and data-protection considerations.
5. `05-ai-governance-risk-assessment.png` — governance-focused AI risk evidence covering explainability, accountability and model-risk concerns.
6. `06-ai-security-risk-assessment.png` — AI security risk evidence covering prompt injection and manipulation of generative-AI outputs.
7. `07-human-oversight-risk-assessment.png` — high-impact AI human-oversight risk demonstrating the need for review, escalation and override mechanisms.

## Evidence Narrative

The selected sequence is designed to show a complete Responsible AI assessment story:

**AI inventory → critical use cases → fairness and bias risk → privacy exposure → governance and explainability → AI security threats → human oversight**.

This evidence demonstrates the ability to classify AI systems, identify high-impact risk, apply likelihood-impact scoring, document decision rationale and assess governance, security, privacy and human-control concerns across AI-enabled services.

## Evidence Handling Note

Only completed, readable and decision-relevant screenshots are retained. Duplicate forms and repetitive entry screens were intentionally excluded so the evidence set remains concise, credible and recruiter-friendly.
