# Project 06 Evidence Index — CriticalOps OT/SCADA BCDR

This evidence index documents the final curated screenshots retained for the portfolio case study. The evidence originates from an educational GRC simulation and is presented as training evidence, not production audit evidence.

## Final Recruiter-Facing Evidence Set

1. `01-asset-inventory-summary.png` — completed asset inventory summary showing five production assets, all classified Critical.
2. `02-risk-register-summary.png` — consolidated risk register showing four open risks across Critical, High and Medium severity.
3. `03-ransomware-critical-risk.png` — ransomware risk assessment scored 4 × 5 = 20, Critical.
4. `04-backup-failure-high-risk.png` — backup failure risk assessment scored 3 × 5 = 15, High.
5. `05-backup-recovery-control.png` — ISO/IEC 27001 A.8.13 backup and recovery control, rated Effective.
6. `06-redundancy-effectiveness-gap.png` — ISO/IEC 27001 A.8.14 redundancy control, rated Partially Effective, demonstrating a control-effectiveness gap.

## Evidence Narrative

The selected sequence is intentionally concise and tells the project story in the order a recruiter or hiring manager is most likely to review it:

**critical assets → consolidated risks → highest-priority threat → recovery weakness → implemented recovery control → residual resilience gap**.

This sequence demonstrates asset criticality, risk prioritisation, likelihood-impact scoring, ISO/IEC 27001 control mapping and the ability to identify residual risk where a control is only partially effective.

## Evidence Handling Note

Only completed, readable and decision-relevant screenshots are retained in the final recruiter-facing evidence set. Duplicate forms, transitional screens and lower-value screenshots were intentionally excluded to keep the case study concise and defensible.
