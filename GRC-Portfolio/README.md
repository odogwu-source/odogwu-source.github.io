## Skills Demonstrated
Risk assessment · risk registers · control mapping · third-party risk · privacy governance · audit readiness · evidence management · remediation tracking · incident-response governance · business continuity · disaster recovery · OT/SCADA resilience · AI governance · model risk · IAM · privileged access · Zero Trust · policy governance

## Portfolio Coverage
The eight projects intentionally cover different GRC domains rather than repeating one compliance exercise: cloud and ISO 27001 risk, third-party/PCI DSS risk, SOC 2 readiness, GDPR privacy, incident-response governance, OT/SCADA resilience and BCDR, AI governance, and banking Zero Trust/IAM risk.
