# Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Portfolio

Eight evidence-backed cybersecurity governance, risk and compliance case studies demonstrating risk assessment, control analysis, evidence evaluation, governance documentation and remediation planning across enterprise, cloud, privacy, third-party, resilience, AI and identity domains.

> **Portfolio note:** Each project is an educational/simulated assessment designed to demonstrate analyst methodology and defensible security decisions. The work is not presented as production client, audit or certification-assurance experience.

## Recruiter Quick Start

For a short review, start with these three projects because they demonstrate different GRC competencies:

1. **[TechStart — ISO 27001 Cloud Risk Assessment](./01-TechStart-ISO27001-Cloud-Risk-Assessment/)** — foundational risk assessment, likelihood/impact scoring, treatment decisions and cloud-control reasoning.
2. **[CriticalOps — OT/SCADA BCDR](./06-CriticalOps-OT-SCADA-BCDR/)** — operational resilience, ransomware risk, backup/recovery, critical-system continuity and disaster-recovery governance.
3. **[SecureBank — Zero Trust, IAM & Access-Control Risk](./08-SecureBank-Zero-Trust-IAM-Risk/)** — identity governance, privileged access, authentication controls and Zero Trust risk analysis.

## Core Capabilities Demonstrated

- Risk identification, analysis and treatment
- Asset classification and risk-register development
- Likelihood/impact scoring and prioritisation
- Control design and control-gap assessment
- Evidence evaluation and audit-readiness reasoning
- Third-party/vendor risk management
- Privacy and compliance governance
- Incident-response governance
- Business continuity and disaster recovery

- Responsible AI governance
- IAM, privileged access and Zero Trust
- Remediation planning and management communication

## Portfolio Coverage

The eight projects deliberately span different GRC domains rather than repeating one exercise: ISO/IEC 27001 cloud risk, third-party/PCI DSS risk, SOC 2 readiness, GDPR privacy, incident-response governance, OT/SCADA resilience and BCDR, Responsible AI governance, and banking Zero Trust/IAM risk.

## Evidence Structure

Each project README explains the scenario, objectives, methodology, findings, risk/control analysis, recommendations and lessons learned. Projects 06–08 also use dedicated `EVIDENCE.md` indexes and curated `evidence/` directories; Projects 01–05 retain their original numbered screenshot-evidence directories.

The evidence is organised to demonstrate the analytical sequence rather than simply prove that a lab was completed.

## Evidence & Ethics Statement

All organisations, systems, findings and risk scenarios in these case studies are fictional or training-derived unless explicitly stated otherwise. Portfolio artefacts demonstrate learning and analytical capability. No project is represented as a production audit, consulting engagement, certification assessment, penetration test or security assurance performed for a real organisation.
