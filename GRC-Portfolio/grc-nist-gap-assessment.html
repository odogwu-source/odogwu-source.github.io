GRC PORTFOLIO • LAB PREPARATION

NIST CSF Security Controls & Gap Assessment

A practical governance and controls case study designed to evaluate a simulated environment against the NIST Cybersecurity Framework and turn identified gaps into prioritized remediation actions.

Objective

Evaluate cybersecurity practices across the NIST CSF functions and identify weaknesses that create operational or business risk.

Planned Deliverables

  • Current-state assessment
  • NIST CSF function mapping
  • Control-gap register
  • Priority ratings
  • Remediation roadmap
  • Executive summary

Hands-on Evidence

Pending practical lab completion. Evidence will be added only after the corresponding controls and observations are validated during training.

Assessment Workflow

Establish scope → review current practices → map evidence to Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover → identify gaps → assess risk → prioritize remediation.

Portfolio Outcome

The final project will demonstrate the ability to connect technical security observations with governance requirements and actionable control improvements.

Status

Project framework prepared; hands-on assessment pending.
