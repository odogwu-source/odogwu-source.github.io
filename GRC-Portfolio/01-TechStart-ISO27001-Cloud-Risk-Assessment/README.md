# TechStart — ISO 27001 Cloud Risk Assessment

## Project Overview
An educational GRC simulation demonstrating a structured cloud-security risk assessment using ISO/IEC 27001 principles. The project focuses on identifying technology risks, evaluating their business impact, documenting treatment decisions and retaining evidence for governance and audit review.

> **Portfolio note:** This is an educational/practice simulation for skills demonstration, not a production compliance certification or audit opinion.

## Objectives
- Establish a repeatable risk-assessment workflow.
- Identify cloud and information-security risks affecting business assets.
- Assess likelihood and impact to determine risk priority.
- Document risk ownership and treatment actions.
- Connect risk decisions to relevant security controls.
- Preserve evidence showing the assessment process.

## Methodology
The exercise follows a practical risk lifecycle: establish context, identify assets and threats, analyse likelihood and impact, evaluate risk, select treatment, assign ownership and monitor residual risk. This demonstrates the relationship between risk assessment and control selection rather than treating compliance as a checklist.

## GRC Skills Demonstrated
- ISO/IEC 27001 risk-management concepts
- Cloud risk identification
- Risk analysis and prioritisation
- Risk-register documentation
- Control selection and mapping
- Risk treatment planning
- Ownership and accountability
- Evidence management and audit readiness
