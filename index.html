ABOUT

Technical security, governance and human-centred thinking.

Hi, I’m Michael Nweke, a cybersecurity professional focused on Security Operations (SOC), threat detection and incident investigation, Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC), and cybersecurity awareness.

My approach is shaped by an academic background in Philosophy and Theology — disciplines that sharpened my critical reasoning, ethical analysis and understanding of human behaviour. I bring this human-centred perspective to cybersecurity, particularly in security awareness, social engineering defence, GRC and cyber ethics, where technology, people, policy and responsibility increasingly intersect.

I hold CompTIA Security+, Network+, CySA+ and Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) credentials, backed by hands-on experience investigating security alerts, analysing suspicious activity, assessing risk, and documenting findings and remediation.

This portfolio documents that work through SOC investigation case studies, GRC projects, security-awareness initiatives and evidence-based technical labs. My aim isn’t simply to demonstrate knowledge of cybersecurity concepts, but to show how I apply them to realistic scenarios — investigating threats, evaluating risk, and communicating security issues clearly.

I bring together technical security expertise, governance and risk knowledge, critical thinking and a human-centred perspective to contribute to security monitoring, incident detection and response, risk management, and organisational cyber resilience.

Lab and simulation work is labelled transparently as educational evidence and is not presented as production client, audit or assurance experience.
