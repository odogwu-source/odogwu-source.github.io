# FinSecure — Third-Party Risk & PCI DSS

## Project Overview
This educational GRC simulation demonstrates third-party/vendor risk management in a payment-security context. It applies risk-based due diligence and PCI DSS-oriented governance to assess how external service providers can affect the security of payment data and business operations.

> **Portfolio note:** Educational/practice simulation only; it is not a PCI DSS assessment or attestation of compliance.

## Objectives
- Identify security and compliance risks introduced by third parties.
- Assess vendor criticality and exposure to payment information.
- Evaluate control and due-diligence evidence.
- Record findings, risk ratings, ownership and remediation actions.
- Support ongoing third-party monitoring and audit readiness.

## Assessment Approach
The project uses a lifecycle view of third-party risk: scope the relationship, identify data/system access, assess inherent risk, evaluate controls and evidence, determine residual exposure, document remediation and maintain monitoring expectations.

## GRC Skills Demonstrated
- Third-party risk management (TPRM)
- PCI DSS governance concepts
- Vendor due diligence
- Risk assessment and prioritisation
- Control-gap identification
- Remediation tracking
- Evidence review
- Risk ownership and accountability
- Compliance documentation

## Key Takeaway
Outsourcing a service does not outsource accountability. Effective TPRM establishes visibility into vendor access, data exposure, control maturity, remediation and ongoing monitoring throughout the relationship.
