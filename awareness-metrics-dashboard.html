Department View — Synthetic

  • Finance: 18% interaction • 68% report
  • HR: 14% interaction • 70% report
  • Operations: 11% interaction • 71% report
  • IT: 5% interaction • 84% report
  • Leadership: 13% interaction • 66% report

Finance and leadership receive priority reinforcement because the simulated scenarios combine higher business impact with weaker reporting behaviour.

Risk Interpretation

  • Completion is not treated as proof of behaviour change.
  • Interaction rate is considered alongside scenario context/difficulty.
  • Reporting rate is treated as an important positive behaviour.
  • Knowledge improvement is compared with behavioural readiness.
  • Department/business impact informs training priority.
