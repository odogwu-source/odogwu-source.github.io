GRC PORTFOLIO • HANDS-ON SIMULATION

DataFlow Analytics — SOC 2 Type II Audit Readiness & Control Testing

A hands-on GRC simulation in which I acted as the Compliance Manager preparing a data-analytics environment for SOC 2 Type II audit readiness. The project covered control documentation, system scoping, evidence collection and operating-effectiveness testing.

Scope

SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria control documentation, in-scope system inventory, evidence-library development and control testing.

Completed to Date

  • Documented 12 SOC 2-aligned controls
  • Catalogued 4 critical production systems
  • Collected 5 audit evidence items
  • Recorded operating-effectiveness tests for logical access controls

Role

Compliance Manager — responsible for organizing controls, owners, evidence and test procedures into an audit-ready structure.

SOC 2 Controls Documented

  • CC6.1: MFA for privileged and administrative accounts.
  • CC6.2: quarterly user-access reviews and prompt termination access revocation.
  • CC7.1: vulnerability scanning and remediation.
  • CC7.2: continuous security-log monitoring and suspicious-activity review.
  • CC7.4: incident-response planning and testing.
  • CC8.1: authorization, testing and approval of production changes.
  • CC6.7: encryption of sensitive customer data at rest and in transit.
  • A1.2: backups and restoration testing.
  • CC9.2: third-party/vendor security assessment.
  • CC2.2: annual employee security-awareness training.
  • A1.3: business continuity and disaster-recovery procedures.

Systems Catalogued

Customer Data Platform

Critical production application used to process and analyse sensitive customer information.

Cloud Data Warehouse

Critical production database supporting customer analytics and controlled data access.

Identity & Access Management

Critical production IAM service supporting authentication, MFA and role-based access.

Security Monitoring & Logging

Centralized monitoring platform supporting audit logs, detection, investigation and incident response.

Evidence Library

Collected simulated audit evidence for MFA enforcement, quarterly user-access reviews, vulnerability scanning and remediation, SIEM/log monitoring, and incident-response plan testing. Each item was linked to its supporting control, owner and evidence location.

Control Testing

MFA Enforcement

Reviewed privileged-account configurations and sampled accounts to verify that MFA was enforced. Test result: Pass.

User Access Reviews

Reviewed quarterly access-review evidence to confirm periodic appropriateness checks and removal of unnecessary access. Test result: Pass.

Termination Access Revocation

Reviewed terminated-user records and account-deactivation evidence to confirm prompt removal of access. Test result: Pass.

Vulnerability Management

Reviewed vulnerability-scan and remediation records to verify risk-based prioritization and timely remediation. Test work documented in the simulation.
