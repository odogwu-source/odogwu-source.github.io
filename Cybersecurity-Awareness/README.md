# Cybersecurity Awareness & Human-Risk Portfolio

A practical security-awareness programme demonstrating how cybersecurity education can reduce human risk through realistic scenarios, clear employee guidance, assessment, measurement and management reporting.

> **Portfolio note:** The programme and example results are educational/simulated artefacts. Synthetic metrics are clearly labelled and are not presented as results from real employees or client engagements.

## Recruiter Quick Start

This portfolio demonstrates the full awareness lifecycle rather than a single training presentation:

**Human-risk identification → Training design → Realistic simulation → Knowledge/behaviour assessment → Measurement → Management reporting → Continuous improvement**

## Professional Value

The programme demonstrates the ability to translate technical cybersecurity threats into guidance that non-technical employees can understand and act on. It also connects training to measurable outcomes and management reporting, which helps position security awareness as a risk-management function rather than a compliance-only exercise.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Phishing and social-engineering education
- Human-risk analysis
- Scenario and simulation development
- Learner knowledge and behaviour assessment
- Security communication for non-technical audiences
- Awareness metrics and KPI design
- Management reporting
- Continuous programme improvement

## Evidence & Ethics Statement

All example organisations, scenarios and results are fictional or training-derived. Metrics displayed in the dashboard are **synthetic demonstration data**. The materials demonstrate programme design and communication capability; they do not claim that a real organisation or workforce was assessed or trained.
