# Cybersecurity Awareness & Human-Risk Portfolio

A practical security-awareness programme demonstrating how cybersecurity education can be structured around realistic human-risk scenarios, clear employee guidance, measurable learning outcomes and management reporting.

> **Portfolio note:** The programme and example results are educational/simulated artefacts. Synthetic metrics are clearly labelled and are not presented as results from real employees or client engagements.

## Included Artefacts
- **Awareness Programme:** programme structure, objectives and delivery approach
- **Phishing & Social-Engineering Scenario Pack:** realistic employee decision scenarios
- **Employee Training Deck:** structured awareness-learning content
- **Awareness Materials Pack:** practical reinforcement materials
- **Knowledge & Behaviour Assessment Pack:** learner understanding and behavioural assessment
- **Synthetic Metrics Dashboard:** demonstration of awareness measurement and trend reporting
- **Management Reporting Pack:** executive-level communication of human-risk findings and actions

## Programme Workflow
**Identify human risk → Define learning objectives → Deliver role-relevant awareness → Simulate realistic scenarios → Assess understanding/behaviour → Measure outcomes → Report to management → Improve the programme**

## Skills Demonstrated
Security-awareness programme design · phishing/social-engineering education · human-risk analysis · scenario development · learner assessment · security communication · metrics design · management reporting · continuous improvement

## Evidence & Ethics Statement
All example organisations, scenarios and results are fictional or training-derived. Metrics displayed in the dashboard are **synthetic demonstration data**. The materials demonstrate programme design and communication capability; they do not claim that a real organisation or workforce was assessed or trained.
