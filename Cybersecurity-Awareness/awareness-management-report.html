PORTFOLIO ARTEFACT 06 • EXECUTIVE REPORTING

Cybersecurity Awareness Management Report

Executive summary for the simulated 250-employee portfolio organisation. Findings and metrics are synthetic and demonstrate how I would translate awareness evidence into risk-based management recommendations.

Executive Summary

The demonstration programme indicates improving employee knowledge and phishing reporting behaviour, while finance/payment workflows and executive-impersonation scenarios remain priority human-risk areas. Training completion is high, but the programme deliberately evaluates behaviour and reporting in addition to completion.

OVERALL DEMONSTRATION RISK: MODERATE • IMPROVING

Key Findings

  • 96% synthetic training completion.
  • Knowledge score improved from 61% to 89%.
  • Simulated interaction rate reduced across three cycles from 24% to 12%.
  • Simulated reporting rate increased from 41% to 72%.
  • Finance and leadership remain priority groups for targeted reinforcement.

Priority Risks

  • Business email compromise and payment diversion.
  • Executive/vendor impersonation.
  • Credential-harvesting lures.
  • Work-relevant HR/document pretexts.
  • Delayed reporting after accidental interaction.

Recommended Actions — 30 Days

  • Reinforce independent payment-change verification.
  • Promote one-click/simple phishing reporting channels.
  • Run targeted microlearning for Finance and Leadership.
  • Remind staff that rapid reporting after a mistake is encouraged.

Recommended Actions — 60–90 Days

  • Run another authorised, safe awareness simulation.
  • Compare reporting and interaction trends by scenario context.
  • Refresh BEC, MFA and credential-protection content.
  • Review reporting procedure and escalation ownership.
  • Present trend and residual human risk to management.

PROGRAMME GOVERNANCE

Management measures

MeasureKnowledge, recognition, verification and reporting behaviour—not completion alone.
ContextualiseInterpret simulation results in light of message difficulty, relevance and employee context.
ImproveUse weak areas to drive targeted microlearning and process/control improvements.
ReportGive management a concise view of human cyber risk, trends, priorities and residual exposure.

Portfolio conclusion

This simulated programme demonstrates an end-to-end awareness lifecycle: define risk → train employees → provide job aids → test with safe scenarios → assess learning → interpret metrics → report risk and recommendations to management.
