METHODOLOGY

Human-centred, risk-based awareness

The portfolio treats phishing awareness as more than a completion exercise. It examines employee recognition and reporting behaviour, message context, scenario difficulty and business impact. Future authorised hands-on exercises can replace synthetic demonstration data while preserving the same measurement framework.

Portfolio integrity

Simulated employee data is clearly identified and is never represented as a real client engagement. Future real organisational training will be documented separately, with permission and appropriate anonymisation.
