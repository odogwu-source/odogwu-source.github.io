SECURITY AWARENESS PORTFOLIO • SIMULATED ORGANISATIONAL PROGRAMME

Employee Cybersecurity Awareness & Social Engineering Defence Programme

A portfolio programme designed to demonstrate how I would plan, deliver, measure and improve cybersecurity awareness for an organisation. The scenario and employee results are simulated for portfolio purposes; completed artefacts will represent work I personally create and analyse.

01PROGRAMME CHARTER

Scope, Audience & Objectives

Define organisational context, target employee groups, human-risk priorities, learning objectives, roles, delivery cadence and success criteria.

  • Simulated 250-employee organisation
  • HR, Finance, Operations, IT and leadership audiences
  • Risk-based training objectives
02TRAINING CONTENT

Employee Security Awareness

Develop practical learning material that employees can apply during everyday work.

  • Phishing, spear phishing and BEC
  • Smishing, vishing, impersonation and pretexting
  • Password security, MFA and credential protection
  • Safe links, attachments and data handling
  • Incident recognition and reporting
03ATTACKER MINDSET

Social Engineering Tactics

Explain the psychological and technical methods attackers use to influence employees.

  • Urgency, authority, fear and curiosity
  • Executive/vendor impersonation
  • Typosquatting and deceptive URLs
  • Credential-harvesting concepts
  • Recognising suspicious requests
04SIMULATION

Authorised Phishing Exercise

Design a safe simulated phishing scenario for awareness measurement, without targeting real people or collecting real credentials.

  • Scenario and target-group rationale
  • Expected phishing indicators
  • Difficulty/context assessment
  • Click/report metrics using synthetic data
  • Post-exercise learning intervention
05ASSESSMENT

Knowledge & Behaviour Measurement

Measure whether the programme improves recognition and reporting behaviour rather than relying on training completion alone.

  • Pre-training knowledge check
  • Post-training assessment
  • Phishing-recognition exercises
  • Reporting-rate analysis
  • Repeat-risk and improvement trends
06MANAGEMENT REPORT

Metrics, Risk & Recommendations

Translate awareness results into an executive-level view of human cyber risk and recommended actions.

  • Completion and assessment performance
  • Simulated click and reporting rates
  • Scenario difficulty/context
  • Priority risk groups and topics
  • Follow-up training roadmap

METHODOLOGY

Evidence-based awareness design

The programme will use recognised human-centred security principles. NIST describes simulated phishing as part of embedded awareness programmes and recommends interpreting click/report results in context rather than treating click rate alone as a complete measure. The portfolio will therefore document scenario difficulty, user context, reporting behaviour and learning outcomes alongside conventional metrics.

Portfolio integrity

No simulated employee data will be represented as a real client engagement. Any future real organisational training will be documented separately and only with appropriate permission and anonymisation.
