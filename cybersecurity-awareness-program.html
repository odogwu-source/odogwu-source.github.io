METHODOLOGY

Evidence-based awareness design

The programme follows human-centred security principles: training should support behaviour change, teach employees to recognise and report social engineering, and measure learning and reporting behaviour rather than relying on completion alone.

Portfolio integrity

No simulated employee data will be represented as a real client engagement. Any future real organisational training will be documented separately and only with appropriate permission and anonymisation.
