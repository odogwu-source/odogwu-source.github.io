GRC PORTFOLIO • LAB PREPARATION

Cybersecurity Risk Assessment & Risk Register

A structured GRC case study prepared for hands-on validation through cybersecurity training labs. This project will document how assets, threats, vulnerabilities and business impact are translated into prioritized security risk decisions.

Objective

Identify important assets and threat scenarios, assess likelihood and impact, determine risk levels, select treatment options and communicate residual risk.

Planned Deliverables

  • Asset and threat inventory
  • Risk register
  • Likelihood and impact scoring
  • Risk treatment plan
  • Residual-risk assessment
  • Management summary

Hands-on Evidence

Pending. Screenshots, lab observations and analyst notes will be added after the practical exercise is completed.

Methodology

Scope → identify assets → identify threats and vulnerabilities → evaluate likelihood and impact → prioritize risk → select treatment → assess residual risk → document findings.

Framework Alignment

The completed case will map relevant findings to appropriate risk and security-control concepts, including NIST CSF and ISO/IEC 27001 where supported by the lab evidence.

Status

Portfolio structure complete. Hands-on validation pending. This page intentionally distinguishes planned work from completed evidence.
