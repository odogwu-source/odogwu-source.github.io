GRC PORTFOLIO • COMPLETED HANDS-ON SIMULATION

HealthTech Systems — Cybersecurity GRC & Incident Response

A completed healthcare cybersecurity simulation connecting incident management, ISO/IEC 27001 control mapping, critical-asset classification and remediation planning. The environment was fictional and designed for practical portfolio development.

Completed Work

  • Documented 3 incident scenarios
  • Defined 8 ISO/IEC 27001-aligned incident-response controls
  • Identified 4 critical healthcare systems
  • Created 5 response/remediation tasks

Outcome

All 4 simulation tasks were completed, producing a coherent incident-to-control-to-asset-to-remediation workflow.

Primary Themes

Ransomware • phishing • data breach • evidence preservation • business continuity • backup and restoration • logging • post-incident improvement.

Incident Register

Ransomware Attack

Critical ransomware incident affecting patient-care systems and creating material availability and recovery risk.

Patient Data Breach

Critical unauthorized-access scenario involving personal and health information and significant confidentiality/privacy impact.

Phishing & Credential Compromise

High-severity phishing scenario resulting in compromised employee credentials and unauthorized account access.

ISO/IEC 27001 Control Mapping

  • A.5.24: incident management planning and preparation.
  • A.5.25: assessment and decision on information-security events.
  • A.5.26: response to information-security incidents.
  • A.5.27: learning from information-security incidents.
  • A.5.28: collection and preservation of evidence.
  • A.5.30: ICT readiness for business continuity.
  • A.8.13: information backup and restoration.
  • A.8.15: security logging and incident investigation.

The frequencies and effectiveness ratings used in the lab are simulated implementation decisions and should not be interpreted as ISO-mandated testing frequencies.

Critical Assets

  • Electronic Health Record (EHR) System — clinical records, diagnoses, treatment information and patient-care workflows.
  • Patient Clinical Database — sensitive patient identities, histories, diagnoses, results and treatment records.
  • Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) — test requests, specimens, results and diagnostic information.
  • Identity and Access Management (IAM) System — authentication and privilege management across critical healthcare systems.

Response & Remediation Workflow

  1. Isolate affected systems and contain ransomware.
  2. Eradicate malicious activity and investigate root cause.
  3. Restore critical clinical systems from verified backups.
  4. Reset compromised credentials and strengthen access controls.
  5. Conduct post-incident review and implement corrective actions.

Skills Demonstrated

Incident governance • ISO 27001 control mapping • healthcare asset criticality • ransomware response • phishing response • data-breach analysis • evidence preservation • backup/recovery • business continuity • security logging • remediation planning.

Portfolio Integrity
